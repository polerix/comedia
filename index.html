NeuroCosmic Spectrum Survey Not a diagnosis. Just a hilarious, cosmic mirror to how your brain perceives this wild universe. Billions and billions of neural fireworks exploding across the cosmos of your mind... This playful survey was forged in the lab of a contemporary psychology PhD student (who genuinely loves neurodiversity), sharpened by the mic of an autistic stand-up comic who has bombed harder at small talk than at open-mic night, and headlined by a journalist from The Onion who once wrote “Local Man Prefers Staring Into Void Over Eye Contact.” Then Carl Sagan himself (or at least his very convincing emulation) wandered in, looked at the draft, smiled, and whispered: “In the vastness of space and the vastness of the human mind, every unique pattern is a gift to the universe.” For entertainment and self-reflection only. No clinical validity. Results may cause spontaneous laughter or sudden urge to info-dump about black holes. 1 The supermarket lights are buzzing like a million angry quantum bees, the carts scream like dying stars, and the PA system is playing smooth jazz at 11,000 decibels. Hyperfocus Harbinger: I lock onto the perfect avocado like it holds the secrets of the universe and completely forget why I came here… until I’m in the parking lot with 17 jars of pickles. Predictability Priest: This was NOT on the approved shopping script! My brain blue-screens. Abort mission or I’ll be recalibrating until Tuesday. Pattern Prophet: I start mapping the exact rhythm of the register beeps to Morse code and suddenly I’m composing a symphony about canned goods. 2 At a party someone says, “The vibes are immaculate tonight!” You reply… Sensory Sentinel: Vibes? Predictability Priest: I had prepared three safe small-talk packets. None of them included “vibes.” Emergency reboot engaged. Pattern Prophet: I immediately see the mathematical beauty of the sound waves and start sketching equations on the nearest napkin. 4 Your favorite coffee shop suddenly rearranges the entire seating layout and replaces your usual barista with someone who smiles too much.
